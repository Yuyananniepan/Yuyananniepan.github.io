## Research Grants, Projects, and Awards

* **$2.5 Million DOE Grant for Emergency Responder Training on MHD ZEVs**

  Dr. Pan served as the **primary proposal writer** and helped **Dr. Xianbiao (XB) Hu** obtain a [$2.5 million grant from the U.S. Department of Energy](https://www.energy.gov/eere/vehicles/funding-selections-fiscal-year-2024-technology-integration-funding-program).

  This project focuses on developing **training programs for emergency responders** who work with **medium- and heavy-duty zero-emission vehicles (MHD ZEVs)**. The project aims to enhance safety, operational readiness, and public confidence in ZEV technology through immersive and interactive training methods.

* **NSF Project on Autonomous Vehicles for Resilient Evacuation Planning**

  Dr. Pan served as the **primary proposal writer** and helped **Dr. Qianwen (Vivian) Guo** obtain a **National Science Foundation (NSF)** funded project, **“Planning: Leveraging Autonomous Vehicles for Resilient and Efficient Evacuation Planning.”**

  This project explores the potential of **autonomous vehicles (AVs) to enhance evacuation planning**, with a focus on improving transportation system resilience, evacuation efficiency, and mobility management during emergencies and disasters.

* **NSF Project on Multi-Layer Infrastructure Resilience under Hurricanes**

  Dr. Pan serves as a **Co-PI** on the **National Science Foundation (NSF)** supported project, **“Proposed Activity Group: Multi-Layer Infrastructure Network Response and Resilience under Hurricane Disasters”**

  This project investigates the **response and resilience of multi-layer infrastructure networks under hurricane disasters**, with a focus on infrastructure interdependencies, disruption propagation, and system-level resilience.

* **Winner of the INRIX MetroLab Challenge**

  Dr. Pan is a **winner of the INRIX MetroLab Challenge** for the project, **“Empirical and Predictive Modeling of Network-Level Resilience under Hurricane-Fueled Wildfire Risk: An INRIX-Based Empirical Study in Florida”**

  This project leverages **INRIX mobility data** to empirically characterize and predict **transportation network resilience under hurricane-fueled wildfire risk in Florida**, with a focus on network disruption, mobility response, and post-disaster recovery.

* **USDA Project on Sustainable Agri-food Supply Chain Leadership**

  Dr. Pan contributes to the **U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA)** funded project led by PI Dr. Qianwen (Vivian) Guo, **“Pathways to Power: Fostering Sustainable Agri-food Supply Chain Management Leadership Among Women and Minorities”** 

  This project focuses on advancing **sustainable agri-food supply chain management and leadership development**, with an emphasis on strengthening education, professional development, and leadership opportunities for women and underrepresented groups.

* **PennDOT Project on Connected and Automated Vehicles**

  Dr. Pan contributed to the **Pennsylvania Department of Transportation (PennDOT)** funded project led by PI Dr. Xianbiao (XB) Hu, **“Act 130 Reporting: Evaluating the Impact of Connected and Automated Vehicles in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania”**
  
  This project evaluates the **development, deployment, and impacts of connected and automated vehicles (CAVs) in Pennsylvania**, supporting PennDOT's Act 130 reporting and providing insights into the implications of emerging vehicle technologies for Pennsylvania's transportation system.
